# True Gain Brand Guide

## Brand position

True Gain is a premium private coaching brand helping adults aged 30–60 build strength, move confidently and stay capable for life.

## Mission

Deliver evidence-led 1:1 strength coaching and sports massage through focused private-studio appointments.

## Brand promise

**Build strength with purpose. Move better. Perform for life.**

## Audience

- Adults aged 30–60
- Professionals and business owners
- Parents
- People returning to training
- Clients who value quality, privacy and accountability

## Personality

True Gain should feel:

- Professional
- Calm
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Evidence-led
- Personal
- Modern

It should never feel:

- Aggressive
- Intimidating
- Cheap
- Gimmicky
- Influencer-led
- “No pain, no gain”

## Colour palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary background | Deep Black | `#050505` |
| Secondary background | Charcoal | `#111111` |

| Highlight accent | Warm Gold | `#E5BD72` |
| Main text | Soft Ivory | `#F4F0E8` |
| Muted text | Stone Grey | `#AAA59D` |

## Tone of voice

Use language that is:

- Clear
- Confident
- Professional
- Encouraging
- Evidence-led

Preferred phrases:

- Build strength with purpose.
- Move with confidence.
- Perform for life.
- Coaching designed around real life.
- Stay capable for the long term.

Avoid:

- Smash your limits.
- No excuses.
- Beast mode.
- Punishment-based language.

## Photography direction

Every website image should feel like it came from the same premium photoshoot:

- Dark private studio
- Warm cinematic lighting
- Green turf
- One client at a time
- Genuine effort
- Premium equipment
- Clean backgrounds
- Minimal clutter
- Consistent colour grading

## Brand North Star

Every interaction should make a prospective client think:

> This feels like a premium private coaching studio where I will receive individual attention and expert guidance.
